CSV files

r = distance from the beam, z = position along it

The end-on view shows the half at negative z, cut at z = 0. The pieces are drawn from the channel positions in the files' extent/ and inventory/ folders (CMSSW 2024 geometry, run 383631). Layer thicknesses, calorimeter depths, the z of the endcap RPC rings and the solenoid are approximate. The iron return yoke is left out. Source and instructions: github.com/sgnoohc/cms-raw-csv.
